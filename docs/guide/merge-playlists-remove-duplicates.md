# Merge playlists and remove duplicates

Besides syncing, Trackhop's **Tools** tidy the playlists you already have, on the service they
live on.

## Merge, split, sort, shuffle

*Tools → New playlist from…*: pick one or more playlists and make a new one from them: **merged**
(optionally keeping one copy of each song), **split** into parts of the size you choose, **sorted**
(by artist, title, album, year, date added or length, or reversed) or **shuffled**. The originals
are not touched.

## Remove duplicates

*Tools → Remove duplicates* finds the songs that appear twice in a playlist (the same
track, the same ISRC, or the same title and artists), while a live or remixed version stays a song of
its own, and removes the extra copies after you check the list.

## And more

- *Repeats across playlists*: the songs that sit in several playlists, and liked songs in none.
- *Unplayable tracks*: the greyed-out ones, with a [playable version to swap in](unplayable-tracks).
