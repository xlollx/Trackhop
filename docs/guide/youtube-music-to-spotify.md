# Transfer YouTube Music playlists to Spotify

Trackhop moves your YouTube Music playlists and liked songs to Spotify and can **keep them in sync**
afterwards, by hand or on a schedule. Free and open source, with no Trackhop account: everything
runs on your phone or your computer.

## What you need

- [Trackhop](https://github.com/xlollx/Trackhop/releases/latest) on Android 8.0 or newer (also on
  [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.xlollx.songport/)), or [Trackhop Desktop](desktop) for
  Windows, macOS and Linux, or the [Docker server](docker).
- Your Google account for YouTube Music and a Spotify account (free or Premium).

## Step by step

1. **Connect YouTube Music** (*Accounts → Add connector → YouTube Music*): sign in on Google's page.
2. **Connect Spotify**.
3. **One playlist:** *Syncs → New sync*, source *YouTube Music* and the playlist (or *Liked music*),
   target *Spotify* and *create a new playlist*. **Many at once:** *Tools → Transfer many
   playlists*.
4. **Preview**, run, and check the **review** for anything Spotify does not have.

## Good to know

- YouTube Music mixes songs and videos: a video's title is read as "artist - title" to find the
  song on Spotify, and doubtful matches go to the review instead of being added.
- A scheduled sync keeps Spotify up to date as the YouTube Music playlist grows; **mirror removals**
  follows deletions too.
- The other way round: [Spotify to YouTube Music](spotify-to-youtube-music).
