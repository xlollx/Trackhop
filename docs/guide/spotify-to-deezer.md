# Transfer Spotify playlists to Deezer

Trackhop copies your Spotify playlists and liked songs to Deezer and can **keep them in sync**.
Free and open source, no Trackhop account, no limit on the number of tracks.

## What you need

- [Trackhop](https://github.com/xlollx/Trackhop/releases/latest) on Android 8.0 or newer (also on
  [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.xlollx.songport/)), or [Trackhop Desktop](desktop) /
  the [Docker server](docker).
- Your Spotify account and a Deezer account.

## Step by step

1. **Connect Spotify**, then **Deezer** (*Accounts → Add connector*), each on its own sign-in page.
2. *Syncs → New sync*: source *Spotify* and a playlist (or *Liked songs*), target *Deezer* and
   *create a new playlist*. Many at once: *Tools → Transfer many playlists*.
3. **Preview**, run, and check the **review** for the tracks that need a choice.

## Good to know

- Spotify's tracks carry their ISRC code, which Deezer's search understands: most songs land on the
  exact recording.
- The other way round: [Deezer to Spotify](deezer-to-spotify).
