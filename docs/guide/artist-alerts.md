# Alerts for the artists you follow

Tap the **bell** next to an artist in **Discover** (New releases, Concerts, Setlists, Records & merch),
or add one by name in **Discover › Followed artists**. Trackhop then checks them in the background
and tells you what is new in **one notification**: "new single by ADTR, Architects concert in Milan,
new merch from BMTH". A tap opens Followed artists, with the latest news and their links.

## What you hear about

For each artist you pick: **new releases**, **concerts near you** (in the country of your last
search in Concerts, within its time range), **new merch** in their own shops, and **records** (vinyl,
CD, cassette on Discogs and in their shops). The first check only takes note of what is already
out, so following an artist never floods you.

## How it checks

Once a day, or once a week, on any network or only on Wi‑Fi, and never on a low battery
(**Followed artists › Checks**, or the switch in Settings). It uses the same sources and the same
saved answers as the Discover pages, one artist after another and spread over time; Ticketmaster
only with your own key. Notifications come on their own channel, **Artist alerts**, that you can mute
in Android's settings. The artists you follow travel with the set-up file (Settings › Backup).
