# Looking for SongShift on Android?

SongShift moves playlists between music services, but only on iPhone and iPad. On Android,
**Trackhop** does the same and more: free, open source, with no account and no track limit.

- **Moves** playlists, liked songs and saved albums between Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music,
  Amazon Music, TIDAL, Deezer, Navidrome, Jellyfin and Plex.
- **Keeps them in sync** afterwards, on a schedule if you like.
- **Reviews** what was not found, with suggestions, instead of guessing.
- Also on [Windows, macOS and Linux](desktop) and as a [Docker server](docker).

Get it from [GitHub](https://github.com/xlollx/Trackhop/releases/latest) or
[F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.xlollx.songport/), then follow [Getting started](getting-started).
