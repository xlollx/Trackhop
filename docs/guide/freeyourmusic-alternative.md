# A free FreeYourMusic alternative

FreeYourMusic is a polished paid app for moving playlists, with a limited free tier. Trackhop does
the same moves **for free, with no track limit**, keeps playlists **in sync** afterwards, and runs
on your own devices.

| | Trackhop | FreeYourMusic |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free, open source (AGPL-3.0) | Paid plans; limited free tier |
| Ongoing sync | Yes, by hand or on a schedule | On paid plans |
| Your own servers (Navidrome, Jellyfin, Plex) | Yes | No |
| Account | None | FreeYourMusic account |

## Getting started

Install [Trackhop](https://github.com/xlollx/Trackhop/releases/latest) (or from
[F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.xlollx.songport/)), connect your services and create a
sync: [step by step](getting-started).
