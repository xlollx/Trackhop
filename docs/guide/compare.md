# Trackhop, Soundiiz, TuneMyMusic, FreeYourMusic and SongShift compared

All five move playlists between music services. They differ in **where they run**, **who holds
your logins**, and **what is free**. Each has its own page with more detail; this one puts them
side by side.

| | Trackhop | Soundiiz | TuneMyMusic | FreeYourMusic | SongShift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Where it runs | Your Android phone, your computer or your own server | Their servers, in the browser | Their servers, in the browser | An app on your devices | An iPhone and iPad app |
| Account | None | A Soundiiz account | Your music services, through its website | A FreeYourMusic account | See its site |
| Moving playlists for free | All of them, no track limit | One at a time, with a cap on tracks | A limit on free tracks | A limited free tier | See its site |
| Keeping playlists in sync | Free, on a schedule, removals too if you want | One sync on the free plan, more when paid | In Premium | On paid plans | See its site |
| Who holds your music logins | Only your device, encrypted | The service | The service | The service | See its site |
| Your own music server (Navidrome, Jellyfin, Plex) | Yes | Not among its services | Not among its services | No | See its site |
| Source code | Open (AGPL-3.0) | Closed | Closed | Closed | Closed |

The other columns follow each service's own pages as they were when these pages were written;
check there for the current plans.

## Which one when

- **A one-off move from a browser, nothing to install**: Soundiiz or TuneMyMusic do it well.
- **On an iPhone only**: SongShift is made for it; Trackhop has no App Store app (its
  [server page works on iPhone](iphone)).
- **Two services for good, kept identical by themselves**: Trackhop, with scheduled syncs at no
  cost, on your phone, computer or home server.
- **Your own music server** next to a streaming service: Trackhop connects Navidrome and other
  Subsonic servers, Jellyfin, Emby, Plex, Lyrion and Mopidy.
- **Not handing your music accounts to anyone**: Trackhop keeps the logins on your own device.
