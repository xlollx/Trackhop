# Transfer your liked songs

Liked songs are often the biggest "playlist" anyone has, and the one transfer tools limit first.
Trackhop treats **liked songs, favourites and saved albums** like any playlist: it copies them to
another service, with no track limit, and can **keep them in sync**.

## Step by step

1. Connect both services (*Accounts → Add connector*).
2. *Syncs → New sync*: as the source pick **Liked songs** (or *Favourites*, *Saved albums*); as the
   target pick the other service's **Liked songs**, or a new playlist if you prefer.
3. **Preview**, run, and check the **review**.

## Good to know

- Thousands of liked songs are fine: the search runs in the background and the app shows its
  progress; the review lists what was not found.
- On YouTube Music "Liked music" can hold videos too: they are read as "artist - title".
- Followed artists can be synced as well.
