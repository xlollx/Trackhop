# Organise your syncs: favourites, labels and tags

A handful of syncs fits on one screen. Twenty or fifty don't: the weekly playlists, the backups,
the ones for the car and the gym. Trackhop has three ways to group them, and a few ways to show
more of them at once.

## Favourites

In a sync's **⋮** menu, **Add to favourites** gives it a star. Starred syncs stay at the top of
**Syncs**, and the **Favourites** filter shows only them.

Prefer your own order? Turn off **Settings › Appearance › Favourites always on top**: starred syncs
keep their star and the filter, and can go anywhere in the list.

## Colour labels

Every sync can wear a colour. In its **⋮** menu › **Style**, pick one and give it a name ("Gym",
"Family", "Archive"): every sync with that colour shares the name, and you can search and filter
by it. **Change the label's colour** recolours all its syncs at once. The same dialog chooses how
the card wears its colour: a band, a fading band, a thin outline, a crescent, a floating bar or a
colour wash.

## Tags

In the sync's editor, **Tags** are pairs of your own, such as `genre = metal` or `mood = calm`; the
value can be left empty (`kids`). A sync can have several. The tags you already used are offered
as you type, so the same tag is written the same way everywhere.

## Find a sync

The search on **Syncs** finds a sync by its name, its services, its label or its tags; the filters
narrow the list by status (to fix, errors, running…), service, colour, favourites or a tag. Case and accents
don't matter.

## Order and size

- **Reorder**: hold a card and drag it, or use **⋮ › Move to position…** (handy with TalkBack).
- **Card size**: **Settings › Appearance › Card size** makes the cards Large, Medium or Small.
- **Minimal view**: the syncs as small tiles in a grid, with their services, name and status. Tap a
  tile for the whole card; hold it for its menu, or drag it to a new place.
- **Tablets and phones on their side**: the tabs move to a rail on the side, the cards sit in two
  or three columns, and pages keep a readable width. What you were typing stays when you turn the
  screen.

The **Discover** tab can be arranged too: hold a card and drag it, and **Reset order** puts the
default back. **Settings › Appearance › Images in Discover** turns its pictures off or keeps them to
Wi‑Fi.

## On Desktop and Docker

The web page of [Trackhop Desktop](desktop) and of the [Docker server](docker) has the same stars,
labels and tags, and so do the [API](docker#api), the [command line](cli) and the MCP tools: you can
list the syncs with a tag and run them as a group, for example every sync tagged `mood = gym` before
a workout, from a script or Home Assistant.
