# Transfer Spotify playlists to Amazon Music

Amazon Music has no public way for other apps to add to your playlists, so most tools skip it or
charge for it. Trackhop signs in to Amazon Music on Amazon's own page and copies your Spotify
playlists and liked songs there, and can **keep them in sync**. Free and open source, on Android.

## What you need

- [Trackhop](https://github.com/xlollx/Trackhop/releases/latest) from GitHub or
  [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.xlollx.songport/), on Android 8.0 or newer.
- Your Spotify account and Amazon Music (Prime or Unlimited).

## Step by step

1. **Connect Spotify**, then **Amazon Music** (*Accounts → Add connector*), signing in on Amazon's page.
2. *Syncs → New sync*: source *Spotify* and a playlist (or *Liked songs*), target *Amazon Music* and
   *create a new playlist*. Many at once: *Tools → Transfer many playlists*.
3. **Preview**, run, and check the **review**.

## Good to know

- Amazon Music is reached through its web player's own interface, not an official one: it works
  today, and may need an update when Amazon changes its player.
- Phone only: Trackhop Desktop and Docker do not sign in to Amazon Music.
- The other way round: [Amazon Music to Spotify](amazon-music-to-spotify).
