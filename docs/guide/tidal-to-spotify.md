# Transfer TIDAL playlists to Spotify

TIDAL and Spotify both publish each recording's ISRC code, so Trackhop matches most tracks
**exactly** when it moves your TIDAL playlists and favourites to Spotify. It can then **keep them in
sync**. Free, open source, no account, no track limit.

## What you need

- [Trackhop](https://github.com/xlollx/Trackhop/releases/latest) on Android 8.0 or newer (also on
  [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.xlollx.songport/)), or [Trackhop Desktop](desktop) /
  the [Docker server](docker).
- Your TIDAL account and a Spotify account (free or Premium).

## Step by step

1. **Connect TIDAL** (*Accounts → Add connector → TIDAL*); the app shows each step.
2. **Connect Spotify**.
3. *Syncs → New sync*: source *TIDAL* and a playlist (or your favourites), target *Spotify* and
   *create a new playlist*. Many at once: *Tools → Transfer many playlists*.
4. **Preview**, run, and check the **review** for anything Spotify does not carry.
