Trackhop 1.16 is out: files your way, and a lot more control.

The headline: Trackhop now reads artist and album from file names, from folders, or from patterns you write yourself. In the Android app, on Desktop and in Docker.

Android app 1.16.0
• New card styles, and a bubble when you tap the status dot
• A warning when many syncs hit one service at once, with a one-tap spread
• Qobuz: refused reads are tried again
• "Pt. 4" and "Part IV" now match
• Last.fm loved tracks as a sync target
• Anghami: sign in with Google
• YouTube Music brand channels
• Rate and share Trackhop from inside the app
• GitHub build: a notification when a new version is out

Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) and Docker 1.9.0
• The web API now does all that the page does
• A trackhop command for each action, and the same as MCP tools for AI assistants
• New webhook events: sync.started and sync.failed
• Files your way, with a Try button and zip download
• Last.fm loved tracks, and matching fixes

Free, no subscription. Every download is at trackhop.org

Thank you: much of this release started as your ideas and reports. Keep them coming.
