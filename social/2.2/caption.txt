Trackhop 2.2 is here, and 2.2.1 with it.

The headline: pick a service in seconds, see why a song was missed, and never lose a notice.

Android app
• "Which service?": one chooser for streaming, home servers, radio, files and more, with search
• Smarter matching: feat., remixes, Taylor's Version, Deluxe and Remaster editions
• "Why wasn't it matched?" shows the candidates Trackhop looked at, and lets you pick one
• Export the songs your syncs couldn't find: CSV, M3U, text, JSON, a Lidarr list, or a File playlist to sync anywhere (2.2.1)
• Radio stations and Shazam as accounts, each with its own name and colour
• A bell keeps every notification, and a tap opens what it's about
• Deezer Family: choose the profile. YouTube Music shows which profile is connected
• Plex: five stars count as liked songs, Sounds like, and copies for Plex Home users
• ListenBrainz as a target, Apple Music removals, ratings in files, Apprise notifications
• One playlist kept the same on several of your services

Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) and Docker
• Nearly everything the app does, in the browser of your computer or server

Free, no subscription. On Google Play, F-Droid and GitHub, and every download is at trackhop.org

Thank you to every user who sent an idea or a report: much of 2.2 started there. Keep them coming.
