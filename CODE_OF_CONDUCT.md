# Code of conduct

Trackhop follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, keep discussions about the work, and leave out
harassment, insults and personal attacks of any kind. This applies to issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews and every other space of the project.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through a
[private report](https://github.com/xlollx/Trackhop/security/advisories/new) (choose it even when it
is not a security issue), or through the contact on the Google Play listing. Reports are read by the
maintainer only and handled in confidence. Content or people that break this code may be removed or
blocked from the project.
