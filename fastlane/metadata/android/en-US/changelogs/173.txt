Trackhop 2.1.1. A sync on several services gets a card like every other sync: colour, label, star, menu and drag. It shows which service is syncing now and names the one that needs attention. Selecting syncs is easier: Select comes first in the menu and has its own button, and a selection ends only when you end it. TIDAL suggestions show their artists again. More services renew their access by themselves. YouTube Music: one refused song no longer stops the others.
